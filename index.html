About Corbin

Coaching that builds confidence on and off the court

I'm Corbin Grantham — a tennis coach based in Whiteland, IN with over 4 years of experience working with athletes of all ages and skill levels. I hold a Bachelor's in Psychology from the University of Cincinnati, and I coach through a lens of sports psychology — focusing on the whole player, not just technique.

My goal is to eventually pursue a PhD in Sports Psychology. In the meantime, I bring that mindset to every lesson — building mental toughness, confidence, and game IQ alongside the physical skills. My training plans are built around you — your goals, your pace, your game.

  • Developed players at many different levels and ages — beginners through competitive
  • Coaches through a psychology lens — mental game, confidence & focus
  • BS in Psychology, University of Cincinnati (May 2026)
  • Treasurer, University of Cincinnati Tennis Club
  • Aspiring Sports Psychology PhD candidate
